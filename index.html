Care, simply

Every plant ships with a tag like the one above. These three rules cover most of it.

Water the soil, not the leaves

Check one knuckle deep. If it is dry, water until it drains. If it is wet, wait.

Give it a week to settle

Keep a new plant in one spot. Some leaf drop after a move is normal.

Feed in the rains

Use compost or cow-dung manure once a month from June to October.
